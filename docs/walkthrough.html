Flare Indexer Walkthrough

What flare_indexer does between DatasetBuilder.build(index.csv, catalog.csv) and the labeled table, shown one image at a time on the synthetic data from examples/data/. The logic below is the package's logic rewritten in the browser; with the default settings it gives the same 76 positive / 42 negative labels as examples/01_full_disk_single_image.py.

STAGE 1Inputs: two CSV files

The package never reads pixels. It needs a time for every image, and a catalog of flares with their times and GOES classes.

Flare catalog flare_catalog.csv · 8 rows · one row per flare

#start_timepeak_timeend_timeclassflux W/m²region

class → flux is classifier.py FluxConverter.to_flux: letter gives the power of ten (A=10⁻⁸ … X=10⁻⁴), the number multiplies it. Two flares have no region.

Image index full_disk_image_index.csv · 118 rows · one row per image

#timestampimage_path

Hourly from 2024-03-01 00:00 to 2024-03-05 23:00, except 2024-03-02 05:00 and 06:00, which are missing on purpose. Any extra column (here image_path) is carried through to the output.

STAGE 2Settings: the five labeling decisions

Every choice is an explicit DatasetBuilder(...) parameter. Change one and stages 3 and 4 recompute.

STAGE 3One image at a time: find flares in the window, reduce, label

This is the loop inside builder.py _build_single_image. Move through the 118 images; the band is the prediction window after image time t, the markers are flares placed at their event_time.

STAGE 4Output: the same table plus a label column

Nothing is removed or reordered. The researcher saves this as CSV and reads image_path + label from PyTorch or TensorFlow.
